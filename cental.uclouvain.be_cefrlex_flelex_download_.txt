 French in receptive context · CEFR levels: A1 A2 B1 B2 C1 C2

 Number of entries
 14,236 lemmas

 Tagger used

 TreeTagger (Schmid, 1994)

 Includes multiword expressions
 No

 Recommended use

 For educational purposes, since this resource transforms the distributional information of each word into a CEFR level. Since the tagset is the one used by TreeTagger, you can also use it in NLP tasks.

 with TreeTagger parts of speech

 Number of entries
 14,236 lemmas

 Tagger used

 TreeTagger (Schmid, 1994)

 Includes multiword expressions
 No

 Recommended use
 For NLP purposes, since the POS-tagset of FLELex-TT is the same as that of the TreeTagger.
 It is thus very easy to automatically analyse a text using TreeTagger and FLELex.
